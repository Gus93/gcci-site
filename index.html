Immediate priority

These steps apply to most online harassment, impersonation, fraud, sextortion, and digital abuse situations.

1

Save evidence

Screenshot everything: profiles, messages, URLs, timestamps, phone numbers, emails, wallet addresses, receipts. Anything you might need later.

2

Do not engage

Don't argue with them, don't tell them you're reporting them, and don't pay anything else hoping it'll fix things. Engagement is what they want.

3

Secure accounts

Change passwords on anything they might have touched. Turn on 2FA. Look at your account recovery email and phone. Check what devices are signed in.

4

Report it

File at IC3, the FTC, and inside the platform where it happened. Sooner is better. Paper trails matter.
