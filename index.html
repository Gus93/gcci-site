I’m Not Sure / Multiple Things

Your situation overlaps several categories.

Harassment, Doxxing, or Threats

If someone is targeting you, exposing personal information, stalking you, or making threats online:

Start with:

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

Also report the abuse on the platform where it is happening. If you feel unsafe or believe there is immediate danger, contact local law enforcement.

Impersonation or Fake Accounts

If someone is pretending to be you or using fake profiles to mislead, harass, or damage your reputation:

Start with:

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

Then report the fake account directly on the platform where it appears.

Cryptocurrency Scam or Financial Fraud

If you sent money or cryptocurrency, were lured into a fake investment, or interacted with a fraudulent wallet or exchange:

Start with:

Chainabuse

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

FTC Fraud Reporting

SEC Complaint Center

CFTC Complaint Portal

Identity Theft or Account Takeover

If someone has compromised your personal information, taken over an account, or used your identity without permission:

Start with:

IdentityTheft.gov

Secure your email, financial accounts, and any connected services immediately.

Sextortion or Non-Consensual Images

If someone is threatening to release intimate content, coercing you, or sharing private images without consent:

Start with:

NCMEC CyberTipline if this involves a minor

StopNCII.org for adults concerned about non-consensual intimate images

Cyber Civil Rights Initiative

I’m Not Sure / Multiple Things Are Happening

If your situation overlaps several categories, start with the broadest official reporting channel while preserving all evidence.

Start with:

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)

What to do next

Four steps that apply no matter which kind of incident you’re dealing with.

1

Preserve Evidence

Screenshots, URLs, usernames, timestamps, emails, wallet addresses, phone numbers.

2

Secure Yourself

Change passwords, enable 2FA, review recovery options, check connected devices.

3

Report It

Use official reporting channels and platform abuse tools as soon as possible.

4

Escalate If Needed

If the situation continues, worsens, or causes harm, professional help may be needed.

If you are in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement immediately.
