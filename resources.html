Resource library

Where to actually get help.

A curated map of the resources cybercrime victims need most often — for image takedowns, reporting channels, identity-theft recovery, free OSINT tools, and crisis support. Everything links to the canonical primary source, not an affiliate.

If you're in immediate crisis

911Emergency · US

Active physical danger, threats of violence, or a situation that needs immediate intervention. Call before doing anything else.

988Suicide & Crisis Lifeline · US

If you're having thoughts of self-harm. Call or text 988 — 24/7, free, confidential. The lifeline now connects routinely with cybercrime-related distress.

Text HOME to 741741Crisis Text Line · US/UK/Canada

If a call feels like too much. A trained counselor texts you back within minutes. Free.

1-800-799-7233Domestic Violence Hotline · US

If the cybercrime is connected to an intimate partner — surveillance, threats, image-based abuse from an ex, etc.

Intimate images leaked or threatened

StopNCII.orgAdults · Free

The canonical tool for adult image-based abuse takedown. You generate a hash from the image on your own device — the image never leaves your phone or computer — and major platforms (Meta, X, TikTok, Reddit, Bumble, Pornhub, many others) scan against the hash to block uploads. Run by Revenge Porn Helpline UK.

Take It Down (NCMEC)Under 18 · Free

Same hash-based approach as StopNCII but operated by the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, specifically for anyone who was a minor when the image was taken.

CCRI Crisis Helpline · 1-844-878-2274Adults · US

Cyber Civil Rights Initiative — 24/7 victim helpline staffed by people trained specifically in image-based abuse. They can walk you through legal options state-by-state.

Reporting cybercrime

FBI IC3 · ic3.govFederal · US

The Internet Crime Complaint Center. File a report for any internet-enabled crime — fraud, sextortion, BEC, crypto scams. Reports get aggregated and the FBI sometimes acts on high-value patterns. Always file here even if nothing visible happens.

FTC · reportfraud.ftc.govFederal · US

The Federal Trade Commission's consumer fraud reporting portal. Different mandate than IC3 — FTC focuses on patterns of deceptive business practice. Report both; they serve different purposes.

IdentityTheft.govFederal · US

The official FTC recovery walkthrough for identity theft. It generates a personalized recovery plan and the forms you need to send to credit bureaus, the IRS, etc. Free.

SSA OIG · oig.ssa.gov/reportFederal · US

Report misuse of your Social Security number directly to the Social Security Administration's Office of the Inspector General.

State Attorney General officesState · US

Most state AGs have a consumer fraud division that handles complaints federal agencies don't. Useful for in-state perpetrators or merchant fraud.

Identity theft and credit fraud

AnnualCreditReport.comFree · US

The only official source for free credit reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. Now offers weekly reports. Use this; ignore any "free" credit-monitoring service that asks for a card.

Place a fraud alert · EquifaxFree · 1 year

A fraud alert is the fastest free protection — sets a flag on your credit so lenders must verify your identity before opening new accounts. Calling one bureau notifies all three.

IRS Identity Protection · IP PINFree · US

If your SSN was exposed, request an IRS Identity Protection PIN — a six-digit number that must be on your tax return for the IRS to accept it. Prevents fraudulent returns being filed in your name.

Free OSINT and verification tools

WhatsMyName.appUsername search

Runs a username across 200+ sites at once. Best free tool to check whether a handle is unique to one platform or part of a connected web of accounts.

haveibeenpwned.comBreach check

Type in an email — see which known data breaches it appeared in. Useful for understanding what information about you is already out there.

TinEyeReverse image

Reverse image search that surfaces the oldest known instance of a photo. Best free tool for confirming whether a profile picture was lifted from somewhere else (catfish detection).

Yandex ImagesReverse image

The reverse-image engine catfish researchers actually rely on. More generous about matching imperfect face crops than Google. Especially good for European faces.

EmailRepEmail reputation

Free tier shows whether an email address has a reputation, has been seen in spam patterns, and rough age/profile. Helpful for vetting unknown senders.

Support communities and advocacy

AARP Fraud Watch Network · 1-877-908-3360All ages · US

Despite the AARP branding, the helpline is open to all ages. Trained volunteers listen, validate, and point at concrete next steps. Especially good for older relatives but useful for anyone.

Identity Theft Resource Center · 1-888-400-5530US

Free advisors who walk you through identity-theft recovery step by step. Nonprofit. The ones to call when IdentityTheft.gov's flow leaves you with questions.

RAINN · 1-800-656-HOPESexual coercion survivors

If sextortion or image-based abuse involves elements of sexual coercion, RAINN's trained counselors handle the trauma side, separate from the takedown side.
