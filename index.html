Immediate priority

These steps apply to most online harassment, impersonation, fraud, sextortion, and digital abuse situations.

1

Save evidence

Capture screenshots, URLs, usernames, timestamps, emails, numbers, wallet addresses, and payment details.

2

Do not engage

Avoid arguing, threatening, paying more, or warning the person that you are reporting them.

3

Secure accounts

Change passwords, enable two-factor authentication, review recovery options, and check connected devices.

4

Report it

Use official reporting channels and platform abuse tools as soon as possible.
